El Reto del día

En la vereda Chichalá, cada rincón cuenta una historia: el trapiche de panela, los cafetales de la ladera, las artesanías de nuestras abuelas. Hoy seremos narradores bilingües: escribiremos sobre nuestro territorio usando el inglés para compartir nuestra cultura con el mundo.

Cada grado tiene su propio desafío, pero todos compartimos la misma vereda, el mismo orgullo de ser de Chichalá, y la misma voz que merece ser escuchada en todos los idiomas. 🌿

Conectores para narrar y describir

Usa estos conectores para darle cohesión y coherencia a tus textos:

Función Conector Ejemplo
Adición also and furthermore We grow coffee. Also, we produce panela.
Contraste but however although The road is difficult, however, we love our village.
Causa because since due to We wake up early because we work on the farm.
Efecto therefore so as a result It rained a lot, therefore the harvest was excellent.
Secuencia first then finally First, we pick the coffee. Then, we dry it.

Texto modelo: aprende del ejemplo

Lee con atención este texto narrativo-descriptivo sobre Chichalá. Identifica los conectores y las funciones textuales marcadas en color:

🌄 My Village: Chichalá

I live in Chichalá, a small but beautiful village in Chaparral, Tolima. The landscape is full of green mountains and clear streams. (descripción)

My family grows coffee and sugarcane. Because the weather is perfect for farming, our harvests are always very good. (causa) As a result, we sell our products in Chaparral every month. (efecto)

Life in the countryside is hard work, however, it is also full of joy and community. (contraste) Furthermore, our traditions keep us united as a family and as a village. (adición)

First, every morning begins with a warm cup of agua de panela. Then, we go out to the fields. Finally, at sunset, we gather as a family and share stories. (secuencia)

I am proud of Chichalá. It is not just a place — it is my identity.
